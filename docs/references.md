# Mechanics references

Primary teaching references from David Roylance, MIT OpenCourseWare, Mechanics
of Materials. Links were checked when this starter was prepared. The code and
project briefs here are original teaching implementations; third-party PDFs
are linked, not redistributed.

- [Stresses in Beams](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/resources/mit3_11f99_bstress/): beam bending stresses used in Tiers 1 and 5.
- [Beam Displacements](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/resources/mit3_11f99_bdisp/): load-curvature and displacement methods for Tiers 1 and 5.
- [Shear and Torsion](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/resources/mit3_11f99_torsion/): nominal shear, circular-shaft torsion, and vector moments for Tiers 2–4.
- [Yield and Plastic Flow](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/resources/mit3_11f99_yield/): yielding criteria underlying the Tier 2 von Mises check.

The Tier 3 equal-stiffness bolt distribution follows from force/moment balance
and a rigid plate rotating about the bolt centroid; its derivation is written
in the tier README. Tier 4 follows from geometry and moment equilibrium.
Neither is presented as an implementation of a joint or aerospace standard.

Material properties and allowable loads in the examples are invented teaching
inputs, not values sourced from these references.
